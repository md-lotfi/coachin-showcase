1. About CoachIn and these terms

These terms cover the CoachIn Android application and its showcase website. “We” means the publisher above. The Privacy Policy explains information handling. Applicable store terms also govern downloads and purchases. Nothing here excludes rights or remedies that cannot lawfully be excluded.

CoachIn helps gyms, studios and coaches organize members, prepaid sessions, attendance, recorded payments and training tools. The website describes the product; it is not a cloud gym-management account or payment portal. Features depend on the installed version and compatible hardware.

2. Authority and responsible use

Use CoachIn only with authority to manage the relevant gym and member information. Gyms must provide appropriate notices, establish permissions or another lawful basis where required, protect children's information and follow applicable privacy, consumer and recordkeeping obligations. Installing the app does not obtain member or guardian permission on your behalf.

Keep records accurate, limit access to authorized people and review important session balances, expiry dates and payment entries. Avoid unnecessary sensitive details and payment-card credentials. The app does not verify the authority of the person holding an unlocked device.

3. Permitted use and intellectual property

You may use CoachIn for lawful, authorized gym administration and share information you are entitled to share. You retain responsibility for your gym records and third-party rights in them. The publisher retains rights in the app, brand and website materials.

Do not impersonate another member, falsify attendance or payments, misuse another person's pass, obtain unauthorized records, distribute malicious backups, infringe rights or bypass access controls. These restrictions do not remove activities or rights expressly protected by mandatory law.

4. Attendance, packages and payment records

Compatible NFC identifiers and QR passes help recognize a stored member record; they do not prove physical identity. Passes can be photographed or copied. Supervise the shared device and verify disputed admissions. The app's attendance view shows recorded check-ins and is not a guaranteed real-time occupancy or emergency roll-call system.

The gym sets package prices, session counts, validity and renewal policies and remains responsible for agreements with its members. Review errors or disputed balances before relying on them. CoachIn records amounts received; it does not charge payment cards, transfer funds, settle bank transactions or replace required receipts, accounting or tax processes.

5. Training tools and hardware

Cardio logs, estimated heart-rate zones, fitness estimates and interval timers are informational training aids, not medical advice, diagnoses, monitoring equipment or guarantees of safe exercise. Qualified professionals and participants must make appropriate health and training decisions. Stop relying on an inaccurate estimate or timer and seek appropriate assistance where necessary.

NFC needs compatible devices/cards; camera, files and other optional functions depend on permissions. Speech, wake-lock and timer behavior can be affected by device settings, background restrictions and interruptions. No particular device or uninterrupted background timing is guaranteed.

6. Local records, access and backups

Protect the device with an appropriate operating-system lock and restrict physical access. The coach PIN is a local convenience control; the current application does not encrypt its database or exported backups. Do not treat a shared kiosk or a static pass as a high-assurance identity or authorization system.

Keep suitable copies of important records outside the device and verify recovery. Current backup/export tools copy the database without packaging separate photo files. Local rotation, export or file validation does not guarantee a complete, recoverable backup. Importing a backup replaces local database content; check the source, compatibility and recovery options before proceeding. Uninstalling, clearing storage, device loss or damaged files may cause loss. The publisher does not have a remote copy to restore.

Exports, passes and screenshots shared outside the app are controlled by their recipients. Deleting or changing an app record does not erase their copies. Restoring old records can bring back previously deleted information. Do not send backups, PINs or identifiable member information to support unless a safe, necessary process has been agreed.

It grants the stated entitlement for the supported product; it does not promise perpetual updates, cloud hosting or compatibility with every future device.
