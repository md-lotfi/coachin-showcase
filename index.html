Features

Built around the front desk and the training floor.

Attendance

Turn an Android device into a check-in station.

  • Members check in by tapping a supported NFC card or tag, or by showing a QR pass.
  • Fallback check-in by name and phone when a card is forgotten.
  • Each check-in updates daily attendance and the member's remaining sessions.
  • Use the Coach PIN screen for access to the app.

NFC check-in requires a phone or tablet with compatible NFC hardware.

FAQ

Questions, answered.

What is CoachIn designed for?

CoachIn helps independent gyms, personal trainers, martial arts schools, studios, and coaches manage members, prepaid session packages, attendance, and payment records from an Android device.

Can I use core features offline?

Yes. Member management, check-ins, and payment recording use local storage on your device and work without an internet connection. Store purchases and advertising may need internet access.

Does NFC work on every phone?

No. NFC check-in requires a phone or tablet with compatible NFC hardware and supported cards or tags. Members can also check in with a QR pass or by name and phone.

Can I use QR membership passes?

Yes. CoachIn can create digital membership passes with a QR code that members show at the kiosk to check in.

How are sessions and expiry tracked?

Each package has a session count and a validity period. Every check-in uses a session, and the app shows remaining sessions and the expiry date for each member.

Does CoachIn process payments?

No. CoachIn records the payments you receive so you can review them. It does not charge cards, transfer money, or act as an online payment gateway.

Where is my gym's data stored?

Your member, attendance, and payment records are stored in a local database on your device.

What should I know about backups?

CoachIn includes local backup and export tools. Export regularly and keep copies outside the device, because a lost or reset device can mean lost data.

Which languages are supported?

The app is available in English, French, and Arabic.

Where can I see Premium pricing?

Premium options — monthly, yearly, and lifetime — and their current prices are shown inside the app.

Is CoachIn available for iPhone?

This website presents the Android version of CoachIn, available on Google Play.
